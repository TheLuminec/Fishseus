🐟 Welcome

Fishseus is a modular voice-assistant stack for a motorized Billy Bass fish. Use the sidebar to configure audio capture, speech recognition, LLM, TTS, motor control, and the fish personality prompt. Chat lets you talk to Fishseus directly from the browser.
